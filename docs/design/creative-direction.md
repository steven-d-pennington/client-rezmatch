# Creative direction: RezMatch

Draft for Steven. Nothing here has been shown to the client.

Written on 2026-11-21, before any call, because Steven asked to start with three mockups in three styles. It rests on one sentence about the product and one about the founder. Treat every choice below as a question put to the client in picture form, not as a conclusion.

## What the material tells us

There is no material yet: no logo, no photos, no words from the client, no call. What we have:

- The name. "RezMatch" is short, plain and a little cheeky. It sounds like something said out loud between friends, not a corporate brand.
- The founder is Native and is family to Steven (Steven, 2026-11-21).
- From the fit check (`intake.md`): the existing Native dating sites are generic networks with a Native label, and the complaint about mainstream apps is being exoticized. So the look must not be "Native-themed". No feathers, dreamcatchers, arrows, headdresses, borrowed patterns or desert sunsets. Any art that carries cultural meaning comes from artists the client chooses.

## Rules for these mockups

- Every photo is a labeled placeholder. No stock or generated faces, least of all of Native people.
- Sample members are plainly samples: a first name, an age, and bracketed fields (`[Nation]`, `[Home community]`) for the client to decide. No real nation is attached to an invented person.
- Nothing shown promises a feature. Identity checks, clan filters, blood quantum and payments do not appear, because the client has not decided them.
- All words are drafts. The headline of each look is written in a different voice on purpose, so the client can react to tone as well as style.
- Color and type carry the difference. System fonts only until a look is chosen.

## The feeling, in three words

Unknown until the call. The three looks each bet on a different word: **warm**, **sharp**, **bright**.

## Visitor journey

Assumed: someone hears about RezMatch from a friend, a relative or a social post, opens the link on a phone, and decides in a few seconds whether this is for them and whether it is real. The first screen says who it is for and offers one next step. Below it, a taste of the app in that style: a profile, browsing, a conversation.

## Three directions

| | Name | The idea in one sentence | What you would remember | Type and color, in words |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| C | Big sky | Bright, open and a little cheeky, like the name: big friendly type and bold blocks of color. | Chunky color tiles with thick navy outlines. | Very heavy rounded sans, warm white, cobalt blue and sun yellow, navy outlines. Big pill shapes. |

## Recommendation

None yet, and that is deliberate. With no material from the client, a recommendation would be Steven's taste or an agent's guess presented as theirs. If pressed: C fits the name best, A fits a "serious relationships" product best, and B is the one that looks most like an app people already trust.
